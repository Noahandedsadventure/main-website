About the Channel

Who We Are

Welcome to Noah & Ed's Train Adventures. We are dedicated railfans capturing the raw power and everyday operations of the Canadian National (CN) Railway. Whether it's massive mixed freights, priority intermodals, or local switching jobs, we are trackside to document it.

Noah

Acts as the Lead Editor and channel manager, ensuring the 4K footage looks crisp, color-graded, and properly mixed for the ultimate heavy-haul experience.

Ed

Co-hosts and handles field operations, scouting locations, and monitoring dispatch to make sure we're in the right spot when the heavy haulers roll through.

The CN Sprague Subdivision

The Sprague Sub is a critical artery for CN, moving massive volumes of freight between Western Canada and the United States. We track the action across our favorite hotspots along the line.

Trackside Gear & Comms

Our Scanner Setup

To stay ahead of the trains, we monitor dispatch and road channels using a Radio Shack PRO-89 handheld scanner. Listening for hotbox defect detectors and RTC (Rail Traffic Control) clearances is the best way to know when CN power is throttling up and heading our way.

CN Sprague Sub Frequencies

Frequency (MHz) AAR Channel Usage

161.205 AAR 73 CN Train Standby / Dispatch
