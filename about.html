Meet the Crew

Noah & Ed

Welcome to our channel! We are passionate railfans dedicated to capturing the raw power of heavy-haul freight, primarily focusing on the CN Sprague Subdivision in Manitoba.

Our goal is to bring you high-quality videos of the best rail action we can find, from massive grain trains to fast-moving intermodals.

The Base 2 Crew

The Base 2 Crew is our community of fellow rail enthusiasts. We love connecting with other railfans to share catches, scanner updates, and stories from trackside.

Be sure to join our Discord to become part of the crew!
