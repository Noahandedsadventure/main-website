Who We Are

Welcome to Noah & Ed's Train Adventures. We are dedicated railfans capturing the raw power and everyday rhythm of Canadian National operations across the Sprague Subdivision and beyond.

Whether it's a heavy grain train, a priority intermodal, or a quiet yard move, we document the moments that make modern railroading exciting.

The Base 2 Crew

The Base 2 Crew is our community of fellow rail enthusiasts who love scanner chatter, road channels, and a great trackside photo or video.

We share catches, compare notes, and keep each other posted on where the power is moving next.

Noah

Handles the editing, channel management, and post-production side of the videos to make each catch feel crisp, dynamic, and immersive.

Ed

Leads with field scouting, route knowledge, and the real-world trackside timing needed to get in the right place when the freight comes through.
