Guide to the CN Sprague Subdivision

About the Route

The Sprague Subdivision is a major Canadian National Railway line running through Manitoba. It's known for seeing a massive volume of heavy-haul freight, intermodals, and grain trains moving between Western Canada and the United States.

For railfans like us, it offers incredible action, deep-woods scenery, and the raw power of modern railroading.

Radio Frequencies (Scanner Guide)

To catch the action, you need to listen in. Here are the common frequencies used along the Sprague Sub:

Frequency (MHz) Usage
161.415 CN Road Channel (Main Communication)
161.205 CN Train Standby / Dispatch
160.935 Local Yard switching / RTC

Our Favorite Hotspots

Winnipeg Terminal: Great for catching trains assembling and departing.

Piney, MB: Excellent rural sightlines for fast-moving freight.

Middlebro, MB: The easternmost point before the US border, offering classic wooded scenery.
