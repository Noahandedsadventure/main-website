About the Channel

Who We Are

Welcome to Noah & Ed's Train Adventures. We are dedicated railfans capturing the raw power and everyday operations of the Canadian National (CN) Railway. Whether it's massive mixed freights, priority intermodals, or local switching jobs, we are trackside to document it.

Noah acts as the Lead Editor and channel manager, ensuring the 4K footage looks crisp, while Ed co-hosts and handles field operations to make sure we're in the right spot when the heavy haulers roll through.

The CN Sprague Subdivision

The Sprague Sub is a critical artery for CN, moving massive volumes of freight between Western Canada and the United States. We track the action across our favorite hotspots.

Primary Catch Locations

  • Winnipeg / Symington Yard: The heart of the action. Great for catching trains assembling, yard jobs, and major departures.
  • Giroux, MB: Classic Manitoba countryside. Excellent open sightlines to catch high-speed through-freights.
  • Rainy River, ON: The eastern edge of our typical run, featuring beautiful scenery and border-crossing action.

Trackside Gear & Communications

Our Scanner Setup

To stay ahead of the trains, we monitor dispatch and road channels using a Radio Shack PRO-89 handheld scanner. Listening for hotbox defect detectors and RTC (Rail Traffic Control) clearances is the best way to know when CN power is heading our way.
