The CN Sprague Sub

Heavy Freight Through Manitoba

About the Route

The Sprague Subdivision is a major Canadian National line that carries a heavy mix of grain, intermodal, and manifest freight through the Manitoba countryside. It is one of the most interesting corridors for railfans because it combines open prairie views, deep-woods sections, and steady mainline traffic.

Winnipeg / Symington

A major hub for trains assembling, yard action, and departures with strong CN presence.

Giroux, MB

Classic Manitoba rail scenery with great sightlines for high-speed through-freights.

Rainy River

A scenic eastern reach where mainline action and border-area movement blend together.

What to Listen For

  • • Hotbox detector calls and track occupancy updates
  • • RTC / dispatcher clearances for westbound and eastbound freight
  • • Yard switching activity at terminal points and local crew changes
  • • Intermodal and grain train timing when the line gets busy during peak periods
